    About Metal Library Explorer

    Metal Library Explorer

    Opens Xcode .gputrace captures, .metallib libraries and .metal sources. Disassembles compiled functions on demand, and shows the shading language text for captures that recorded it. Runs entirely in the browser; nothing is uploaded.
